Digital Interactive Gym Trainer

WorkoutPlus Kiosk (WPK) is an innovative digital, AI-powered interactive fitness training solution with gamification features — self-serviced, touchscreen-driven, and built for commercial, residential, and corporate fitness centres.

Inside the kiosk

One touchscreen, the whole session

Step 01 — Arrive

Gym Dashboard

Main Features:

  • New Member can create the enquiry.
  • Top Members are getting displayed.
  • Members can login to there DashBoard by Using Face-Login .
  • Users can View & Excess all Public Workout Contents.
  • Promotional Ads are show in the Banner Section.

The dashboard helps members access gym services, while banner ads promote brands and generate extra income for the gym owner.

Step 02 — Plan

Workout Card

  • Select a date to view the scheduled workout for that day.
  • View the exercise name, type, count, distance, and status.
  • Record sets, reps, and weight in KG or LBS.
  • Edit workout details and check the exercise completion status.
  • Mark workout report & track your progress
Step 03 — Build

Strength Training – Key Feature

  • Watch the video to learn the correct exercise movement.
  • Easily see which exercise you need to perform next.
  • Check which muscles are mainly worked during the exercise.
  • Identify whether it is bodyweight or another type of training.
  • Select different exercises based on your workout plan
    • Step 04 — Log

    Workout Planner

  • View the active workout plan and remaining activities.
  • Check your workout progress using the progress percentage.
  • Move between months and quickly select the current date.
  • View scheduled workout days and rest days on the calendar.
  • Tap a highlighted date to see the workout planned for that day.
